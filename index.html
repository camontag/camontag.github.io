01 — 03MATHEMATICAL PHYSICS
01 / RESEARCH

Understanding dynamics
beyond the adiabatic limit.

A central goal of my doctoral work is to understand how electronic states evolve when an external electric field changes in time—and what is missed when the response is treated as instantaneous.

01ANALYTICAL

Resonance & scattering theory

Using a one-dimensional localized-potential model in an electric field, I derive energy-normalized scattering states and study spectral density and the structure of Stark resonances.

Stark resonancesSpectral analysis
02MATHEMATICAL METHODS

Nonadiabatic continuum coupling

I derive an evolution operator governing overlaps between instantaneous scattering states, providing a framework for describing nonadiabatic coupling across the continuum.

Continuum dynamicsOperator methods
03LONG-TERM DIRECTION

Beyond-adiabatic ionization

This work aims to place the metastable electronic state approach (MESA) on a firmer mathematical foundation and enable the study of post-adiabatic effects in long-wavelength laser fields.

Strong-field physicsLaser–matter interaction
